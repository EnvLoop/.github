# EnvLoop

EnvLoop is a post-training platform for recursive self-improvement (RSI): environments, evaluation and training for agents.

[Website](https://envloop.ai) · [Research](https://envloop.ai/research) · [Docs](https://docs.envloop.ai) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/EnvLoop) · [CLI on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/envloop-cli/)

## Research releases

- **Pev**: a calibrated fast-decision model for personal agents, and the Pev-Bench benchmark. [Paper page](https://envloop.ai/research/pev) · [Leaderboard](https://envloop.ai/leaderboard/pev-bench) · [Code](https://github.com/EnvLoop/Pev)
- **Recursive Play**: 150 executable puzzle environments for agents. [Paper page](https://envloop.ai/research/recursive-play) · [Leaderboard](https://envloop.ai/leaderboard/recursive-play) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/EnvLoop/Recursive-Play-Bench)
- **CUA-RSIBench**: executable data research for verifiable computer use. [Paper page](https://envloop.ai/research/cua-rsibench) · [Code](https://github.com/EnvLoop/cua-rsibench)
